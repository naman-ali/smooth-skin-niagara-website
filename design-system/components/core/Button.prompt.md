Substantial, elegant CTA button in three variants — use `primary` for the single most important action per view (e.g. "Book a Free Consultation").

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" icon={<ArrowIcon/>}>Book Your Free Consultation</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">View Results</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (deep olive fill, warm-white text), `secondary` (outlined, transparent/warm-white), `ghost` (text-only, for tertiary links). Sizes: `md` (default), `sm`. Accepts an optional icon (left or right) — keep icons thin-line and minimal (arrow, calendar).
