A self-contained testimonial card — olive quote-mark glyph, quote body (wrap key phrases in `<strong>`), a hairline divider, author name, then a 5-star olive rating row. Stack 2-3 vertically alongside a bio/portrait section.

```jsx
<TestimonialQuote quote={<>Ashley is <strong>extremely kind, knowledgeable</strong>, and truly amazing.</>} author="Bri McKinnon" rating={5} />
```
