## Your Questions Answered (FAQ)

### Are PCA Skin Peels safe for all skin types?

Yes, PCA Skin Peels are formulated to be safe for all skin types, including sensitive skin. Our trained professionals will assess your skin to ensure the best treatment plan.

### What should I expect during the recovery period?

After a PCA Skin Peel, you may experience mild redness and peeling, which typically subsides within a few days. It’s important to follow post-care instructions for optimal results.

### How soon will I see results from a PCA Skin Peel?

Many clients notice improvements in their skin’s texture and tone after just one treatment, with more dramatic results visible after a series of peels.

### Can I wear makeup after a PCA Skin Peel?

It’s best to avoid makeup for at least 24 hours after the peel to allow your skin to breathe and heal properly. Our team will provide specific aftercare instructions.

### How often should I get a PCA Skin Peel?

For optimal results, a series of treatments spaced 3-4 weeks apart is recommended. However, your skincare professional will tailor a plan to suit your individual needs.

### Will a PCA Skin Peel help with acne?

Yes, PCA Skin Peels can be very effective in reducing acne and preventing future breakouts by exfoliating the skin and unclogging pores.

### Is there any downtime after a PCA Skin Peel?

There is minimal downtime with PCA Skin Peels. Most clients can return to their normal activities immediately, although some may experience mild peeling.

### What should I do to prepare for a PCA Skin Peel?

It’s important to avoid exfoliating treatments and sun exposure before your peel. Our team will provide detailed pre-treatment instructions to ensure the best results.

### Can PCA Skin Peels be combined with other treatments?

Yes, PCA Skin Peels can be combined with other skincare treatments for enhanced results. Consult with our professionals to create a customized skincare plan.
