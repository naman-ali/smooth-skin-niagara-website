I was told about the possible side effects of the treatment including: excessive redness or swelling, itching, irritated skin, scratches, visible capillaries, sensitivity to touch, change of pigmentation, chance of transient skin break out such as pimples, allergic reaction.

Although these effects are rare and expected to be temporary, any adverse reaction should be reported immediately.
